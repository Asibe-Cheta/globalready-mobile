FINAL DEVELOPER IMPLEMENTATION CHECKLIST
GlobalReady Developer Implementation ChecklistPurpose: Legal, checkout, subscription, and disclosure updates required for the live app and website.
1. Update pricing language everywhere
The current live model is:
	•	GlobalReady has a free plan with limited access
	•	GlobalReady Pro costs €9.99 per month
	•	GlobalReady Pro gives access to premium features within GlobalReady
	•	price may increase later with prior notice before the new price applies
Remove all old wording about:
	•	“€5 per service”
	•	“€5 per transaction”
unless that wording still applies to a separate real product and is clearly explained.
All of these must match:
	•	pricing page
	•	upgrade/paywall screen
	•	checkout page
	•	account subscription page
	•	Terms of Service
	•	refund wording
	•	billing emails or receipts
	•	app store descriptions if relevant
2. Add clear Pro plan wording
Use this exact or very similar wording on the upgrade page, pricing page, and anywhere Pro is described:
GlobalReady offers a free plan with limited access and a paid subscription plan called GlobalReady Pro at €9.99 per month. GlobalReady Pro gives users access to premium features within GlobalReady, including full access to CV tools, CV tailoring support, curated job discovery, AI interview practice, and other paid features made available within the platform. Some job opportunities may link to third-party platforms that require separate registration, subscription, payment, or additional application steps.
3. Add outbound job-link disclaimer
Place this notice near job listings or before opening an external job link:
Some opportunities are hosted on third-party platforms and may have their own access requirements, subscriptions, or fees.
This should be visible:
	•	on job detail pages
	•	before redirecting out
	•	near “Apply” or “Open Job” buttons where possible
4. Fix checkout screen disclosures
Before the user pays, show all of the following clearly:
	•	plan name
	•	plan price
	•	billing frequency
	•	whether it renews automatically
	•	how to cancel
	•	what Pro includes
	•	key limitation about third-party job platforms
Suggested checkout disclosure block:
You are subscribing to GlobalReady Pro for €9.99 per month. Your subscription renews automatically unless cancelled before your next billing date. GlobalReady also offers a free plan with limited access. GlobalReady Pro gives access to premium features within GlobalReady, including full access to CV tools, CV tailoring support, curated job discovery, AI interview practice, and other paid features made available within the platform. Some job opportunities may link to third-party platforms that require separate registration, subscription, payment, or additional application steps.
5. Add cancellation instructions
Users must be able to see how to cancel.
Add cancellation information in:
	•	checkout
	•	account settings
	•	billing page
	•	Terms of Service
	•	help/support page
Wording should state the real method, for example:
	•	cancel in account settings
	•	cancel through Google Play
	•	cancel through Apple subscriptions
	•	contact support at contact@globalready.tech
6. Add refund and billing support language
Show users where to contact support for:
	•	billing errors
	•	cancellation issues
	•	refund requests
	•	complaints
Suggested support wording:
For billing, cancellation, or refund questions, contact contact@globalready.tech.
7. Update legal pages
Publish and link the following:
	•	Terms of Service
	•	Privacy Policy
These should be accessible from:
	•	website footer
	•	app settings/profile
	•	checkout/paywall
	•	signup/login screens where relevant
8. Update operator details consistently
Use the same operator identity across:
	•	Terms
	•	Privacy Policy
	•	checkout
	•	billing emails
	•	support pages
	•	website footer/imprint if applicable
Current draft identity format:
[Your Full Legal Name] trading as GlobalReadyMastweg 8342349 WuppertalGermanyEmail: contact@globalready.techWebsite: globalready.com
Do not label this as a registered trade address until the Gewerbe is actually filed.
9. Add AI feature disclosure
Where AI interview practice or AI-generated tools are used, add wording that makes clear the feature is guidance/support, not guaranteed truth or outcome.
Suggested wording:
AI-generated feedback, interview practice, and CV suggestions are provided for informational and training purposes only and may not always be accurate, complete, or suitable for every situation.
10. Add privacy-related controls if available
Where technically possible, provide users with:
	•	account deletion request path
	•	support contact for privacy requests
	•	access to Privacy Policy in settings
	•	marketing unsubscribe option if marketing emails are used
11. Final pre-launch / post-update QA
Before publishing, confirm all of the following:
	•	old €5 wording is removed or correctly limited
	•	Pro price is consistent everywhere
	•	free plan is mentioned correctly where relevant
	•	third-party platform disclaimer is visible
	•	AI interview practice is mentioned consistently
	•	checkout includes auto-renewal wording
	•	cancellation method is clearly shown
	•	Terms and Privacy links work
	•	support email is correct
	•	operator name is correct
	•	address is correct
	•	no false wording suggests all third-party jobs are fully unlocked by Pro

Replace these placeholders:
	•	[Your Full Legal Name]

13. Important note for rollout
If the business is not yet formally registered as a Gewerbe, do not describe GlobalReady as a registered company, UG, GmbH, or registered trade. Use the operator’s personal legal name trading as GlobalReady until registration is completed.
