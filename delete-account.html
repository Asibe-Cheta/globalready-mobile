Delete Your Account

You can delete your GlobalReady account and all associated data directly from the mobile app, or by contacting our support team.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

1
Open the GlobalReady app on your device
2
Go to Profile → Settings
3
Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account
4
Confirm the deletion — your account will be permanently removed immediately

Option 2 — Contact support

1
Email us at support@globalready.tech with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
2
Include the email address associated with your account
3
We will process your request within 7 days and confirm by email
⚠️ Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your CVs, assessments, course registrations, and profile data will be removed.
